About Sosan關於 Sosan

Sosan Hua, Health Investment CoachSosan Hua，健康投資教練

Former registered dietitian and Certified Diabetes Educator, and trusted nutrition voice on Chinese radio for 18 years. Now helping busy Chinese women over 40 reclaim their health.

前註冊營養師及認證糖尿病教育師，十八年來於中文電台分享營養知識。如今專注幫助40歲以上忙碌的華人女性找回健康。

Why I do this我的使命

My grandmother's battle with diabetes and its heartbreaking outcome sparked my passion for health and wellness. I know firsthand how aging and menopause change our lives, because I'm experiencing these changes myself.

我祖母與糖尿病抗爭的經歷及其悲劇結局，點燃了我對健康與養生的熱情。我深知年齡增長與更年期帶來的改變，因為我自己正親身經歷這些轉變。

After discovering functional medicine and mindset coaching, I saw incredible results when we addressed the root causes of health issues and built simple, sustainable habits. As a busy mom, running a private practice was leaving me overwhelmed, so I moved online to reach and help more women without giving up family life.

在發現功能性醫學與心態調整的力量後，我見證了針對健康問題的根本原因、建立簡單而持續的習慣所帶來的驚人效果。作為一位忙碌的母親，經營私人診所讓我倍感壓力，於是我轉向網上平台，在不犧牲家庭生活的情況下幫助更多女性。

I believe investing in your health is as crucial as planning for your financial future. Health is not just about food. It's about nourishing your body, staying active, managing stress and resting well.

我相信，投資健康就如同為財富未來做規劃一樣重要。健康不僅僅是飲食，更關乎如何滋養身體、保持運動、管理壓力和充分休息。
